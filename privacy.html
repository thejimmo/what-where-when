Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy covers "What Where When Uploader", a private tool used only by the owner of the What Where When YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos.

Data the tool accesses

  • With the owner's permission, Google OAuth grants the tool access to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and to read basic channel information (the channel ID and name), which the tool uses to confirm it is uploading to the correct channel.
  • The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any data about viewers or other users.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising or any purpose other than uploading the channel's videos.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact us through the What Where When YouTube channel.
